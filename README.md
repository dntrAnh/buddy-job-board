# Crew Connect

help me build a job board but for a group of people only, they can paste a new job post so that it notify (send alert emails) to other people in the group and tell them to apply. then each person in that group can have their dashboard of what to apply to/ they haven't applied to and then there will be a board to notify whether or not their friends have applied to the job posts in the list to be applied (to remind them to apply) :)
