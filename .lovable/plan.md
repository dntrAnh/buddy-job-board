# Mobile compatibility and simpler group management

## Changes
- Rework the board header, filters, action rows, tabs, cards, and dialogs so controls stack cleanly and remain usable on narrow phone screens.
- Rework group management forms and member rows so links, emails, and actions wrap without clipping.
- Remove the email-delivery section and its data request from group management. Email preferences remain available from the board.

## Validation
- Check the board and group management pages at phone and desktop widths.
